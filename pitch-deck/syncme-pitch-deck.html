02 · Solution

What we built, in two sentences.

SyncMe connects read-only Gmail and your Moodle calendar, and an AI engine extracts every exam, quiz, clinic appointment and residence-permit deadline into one prioritized list — flagging the days when deadlines collide, before they burn you. Everything unworthy never reaches the list: newsletters, social notifications, personal chat — filtered at the query, the gate and the model.

gmail.readonly — can't send, can't delete
Moodle .ics — pasted once, stays in your browser
Zero storage — fetched live, never saved on servers

05 · Impact and next steps

No more missed deadlines — for students who arrived alone.

Who benefits

International students

Residence permits, clinic visits and exam windows — in a second language, inside an admin system they've known for weeks. SyncMe reads it so they don't misread it.

Every Debrecen student

Neptun registration windows and Moodle quizzes stop colliding silently — the radar surfaces the collision days in advance.

The university

Fewer panicked support emails, fewer lost semesters, and a template any campus can adopt.

What comes next

Deadline reminders

We already hold the date and the priority — push notifications 48 hours ahead are one integration away.

Any campus, any Gmail

SyncMe is config-only: point it at another university's domain and it works — no code changes.

Full bilingual interface and one-tap deep links into Neptun and Moodle tasks.
